About Image Compressor

What this site does

Image Compressor is a free online tool for making images smaller without giving up privacy. It lets you compress JPEG, PNG, and WebP images, resize photos to exact dimensions, remove image backgrounds, and process many files at once with a single ZIP download — all from one page.

Browser-based processing

Unlike many online tools, Image Compressor does not upload your files anywhere. All compression, resizing, and background removal happen locally in your web browser using standard web technologies. Your images never leave your device, which means faster processing, no file-size queues, and no risk of your photos sitting on someone else’s server.

Who built it

The site is built and maintained by Masud Rana as a free, lightweight utility. The source code is available on GitHub, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Free to use

There are no accounts, subscriptions, watermarks, or usage limits. In the future the site may be supported by clearly labeled advertising; the tools themselves will remain free.
