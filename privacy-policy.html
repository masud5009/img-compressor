Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Image Compressor (“the site”) handles information when you use it. The short version: your images never leave your device, and we do not collect personal information about you.

Images and files you process

All image compression, resizing, and background removal happen locally in your web browser using JavaScript. The images you select or drop into the tool are not uploaded to our server or any other server, and we have no access to them at any point.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or process personal information. The site does not require an account, does not set cookies for tracking, and does not use analytics.

Cookies and local storage

The site does not use tracking cookies. Your browser may store small amounts of site data (for example, cached files) to make the page load faster; this data stays on your device.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub’s servers may automatically log standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type for security and operational purposes. This is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Third-party services

The page loads open-source libraries (JSZip, the background-removal library) and fonts from public CDNs (jsDelivr and Google Fonts). Loading these resources involves a standard request from your browser to those services; they do not receive your images.

Future advertising: the site may later display ads provided by third-party advertising networks. If enabled, those networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. This policy will be updated before any advertising is activated, and ad spaces on the site are always clearly labeled.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect your data, there is nothing of yours for us to delete or export. You can clear cached site data at any time through your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Questions

Questions about this policy can be raised via our GitHub repository.
